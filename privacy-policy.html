Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026. Your privacy and trust are fundamental to EDU YODHA (eduyodha.com).

At EDU YODHA (accessible from eduyodha.com), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by EDU YODHA and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you interact with our platform, fill out internship registration forms, upload study notes, or contact our support desk:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, email address, phone number/WhatsApp number, college name, academic year, and branch.
  • Submitted Documents: Study notes, handwritten PDFs, lab manuals, or uploaded educational material.
  • Technical Log Data: Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and click counts.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for educational delivery and platform management:

  • To process internship applications, issue offer letters, and manage project assignments.
  • To provide instant WhatsApp circular alerts and academic notifications.
  • To verify student-uploaded study notes and maintain content quality.
  • To monitor website performance, detect security threats, and prevent spam.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

EDU YODHA works with third-party vendors, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website.

  • Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads to users based on prior visits to our site or other websites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.
  • You may also opt-out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Payment & Security

Payments on EDU YODHA are processed securely using Razorpay Live Payment Gateway. We do NOT store card numbers, UPI PINs, or banking passwords on our servers. All payment processing is protected by 256-bit SSL encryption provided by PCI-DSS compliant payment processors.

5. Contact Us Regarding Your Data

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at rcfcreation9@gmail.com or visit our Contact Page.
