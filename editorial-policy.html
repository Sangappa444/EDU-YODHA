Editorial & Verification Policy

How EDU YODHA verifies VTU notices, KCET cutoffs, and educational articles before publishing.

At EDU YODHA, accuracy and trust are our highest priorities. Because thousands of engineering and college students rely on our platform for university timetables, revaluation rules, and KCET admission strategies, we maintain strict editorial standards.

1. Primary Source Verification

Every university circular, timetable alert, or KCET notification published on EDU YODHA is verified against official primary sources:

  • Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU Belagavi) official circular releases.
  • Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) official press releases and seat matrix publications.
  • Direct academic department notifications from affiliated engineering institutes.

2. Original Educational Content Guidelines

Our educational guides, DSA interview sheets, SGPA calculation tools, and career roadmaps are authored by experienced engineering educators and domain mentors:

  • No automated thin content or clickbait headline generation.
  • Every article includes practical step-by-step examples, official formulas, and structured headers.
  • Articles are regularly updated to reflect new scheme regulations (2021 & 2022 scheme amendments).

3. Correction & Moderation Policy

If a university circular is amended or an official date is revised by VTU/KEA, EDU YODHA updates the corresponding notice immediately. If you spot an inaccuracy or wish to request moderation on community-uploaded study notes, email rcfcreation9@gmail.com.
