KCET Option Entry Strategy & Choice 1, 2, 3, 4 Guide

Everything you need to know about Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) seat allotment, college priority list building, and choice rules.

KEA & KCET Counseling Updated for KCET 2026 • By KEA Counseling Experts

Securing a top engineering seat through KCET (Karnataka CET) depends not just on your rank, but on your Option Entry Sequencing. A high-ranking student who enters options carelessly can lose out on top colleges like RVCE, BMSCE, or MSRIT.

1. Understanding KEA Choice Options (Round 1 & 2)

After KEA declares Round 1 allotment, every candidate who receives a seat MUST exercise one of the following 4 Choices within the stipulated deadline:

Choice 1: Satisfied & Accept

You are 100% satisfied with the allotted seat. You pay the fee, download the admission order, and report to the college. You exit further counseling rounds.

Choice 2: Hold & Upgrade

You accept the allotted seat provisionally, pay the fee, and participate in Round 2 to try for higher priority options. If upgraded in Round 2, the Round 1 seat is automatically cancelled.

Choice 3: Reject & Upgrade

You reject the allotted seat completely without paying fee, but wish to participate in Round 2 for higher options. The Round 1 seat is freed up for other candidates.

Choice 4: Exit Counseling

You reject the allotted seat and exit KCET counseling altogether. You will not be considered for subsequent rounds.

2. Golden Rules for Building Option Entry List

  1. Always Put Best Dream College at Position #1: The KEA computer algorithm evaluates options sequentially from #1 downwards. It stops at the first option matching your rank.
  2. Never Put an Unwanted College: Never add a college or branch you are unwilling to join just to fill options. If allotted, you may be bound to it.
  3. Group by Course Priority or College Priority: Decide whether your priority is the Branch (e.g., CSE/AI everywhere first) or College Reputation (e.g., RVCE ECE over tier-3 CSE).
