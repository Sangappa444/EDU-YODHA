VTU Grace Marks & Vertical Progression Backlog Rules

Comprehensive guide explaining university grace mark allocation limits, passing criteria, and maximum backlogs allowed for promotion to higher semesters.

VTU Academic Regulations Updated for 2026 Batch • By EDU YODHA Academic Team

Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU) implements specific ordinance guidelines governing Grace Marks Award and Vertical Progression (Year-Back Rules) to ensure academic standards while protecting student career progression.

1. VTU Grace Marks Rules (OB 3.1 & OB 3.2)

Grace marks are awarded automatically by VTU during result processing under two categories:

A. Grace Marks for Passing a Theory Subject (Max 1-2%)

A student missing the passing mark in a theory subject by up to 1% or 2% of total aggregate marks (max 2-5 marks) is awarded grace marks, provided passing that subject completes all requirements for the semester or degree. Grace marks are NOT awarded for lab practicals or viva.

B. Grace Marks for Class Distinction / First Class

If a student falls short of First Class Distinction (70% / 7.75 CGPA) or First Class (60% / 6.75 CGPA) by a fraction of a mark in aggregate, grace marks up to 0.1% may be added to award the higher class distinction.

2. Vertical Progression (Year-Back) Rules (2021 & 2022 Scheme)

To be eligible to move from lower odd semesters to higher odd semesters (e.g., 3rd Sem to 5th Sem, or 5th Sem to 7th Sem), VTU mandates maximum backlog limits:

  • Promotion to 3rd Semester: No backlog restriction from 1st/2nd sem (P/C Cycle).
  • Promotion to 5th Semester: Student must have cleared ALL 1st and 2nd semester subjects completely (0 backlog in 1st year). Max 4 backlogs permitted in 3rd & 4th semesters combined.
  • Promotion to 7th Semester: Student must have cleared ALL 1st, 2nd, 3rd, and 4th semester subjects completely (0 backlog in 1st and 2nd year).
