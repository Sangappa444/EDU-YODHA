How to Calculate VTU SGPA & CGPA

Complete mathematical breakdown for 2021 & 2022 Scheme (CBCS). Grade point tables, fail credit handling, and VTU percentage conversion formula explained.

VTU Academic Guide Updated for 2026 Batch � By EDU YODHA Academic Team

Understanding how your Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) and Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) are calculated is vital for every B.E. / B.Tech student studying under Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU Belagavi).

1. VTU Grade Point System (2021 & 2022 Scheme)

Under the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS), VTU converts your total marks out of 100 in each course (Continuous Internal Evaluation + Semester End Examination) into a letter grade and numerical Grade Point (GP).

Marks Range (%) Letter Grade Grade Point (GP) Performance Level
90 - 100 O 10 Outstanding
80 - 89 A+ 9 Excellent
70 - 79 A 8 Very Good
60 - 69 B+ 7 Good
55 - 59 B 6 Above Average
50 - 54 C 5 Average
40 - 49 P 4 Pass
Below 40 F 0 Fail

2. VTU SGPA Formula

SGPA is calculated for a single semester using the formula:

SGPA = S (Course Credits � Grade Points Earned) / S (Total Course Credits)

Example Calculation:

Consider a student in 4th Semester CSE with 5 theory subjects and 2 labs:

  • Maths (4 credits): Grade A+ (GP = 9) ? 4 � 9 = 36
  • DAA (4 credits): Grade A (GP = 8) ? 4 � 8 = 32
  • OS (3 credits): Grade B+ (GP = 7) ? 3 � 7 = 21
  • DBMS (3 credits): Grade A (GP = 8) ? 3 � 8 = 24
  • Python (3 credits): Grade O (GP = 10) ? 3 � 10 = 30
  • DAA Lab (1.5 credits): Grade O (GP = 10) ? 1.5 � 10 = 15
  • OS Lab (1.5 credits): Grade A+ (GP = 9) ? 1.5 � 9 = 13.5

Total Earned Points = 36 + 32 + 21 + 24 + 30 + 15 + 13.5 = 171.5
Total Credits = 4 + 4 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 1.5 + 1.5 = 20 credits
SGPA = 171.5 / 20 = 8.575

3. VTU CGPA Formula

CGPA measures your cumulative academic performance across all completed semesters:

CGPA = S (Semester Total Credits � Semester SGPA) / S (Total Cumulative Credits)

4. VTU Percentage Conversion Formula

To convert your official VTU CGPA into percentage for job applications or higher studies:

Equivalent Percentage (%) = (CGPA - 0.75) � 10

Example: If your CGPA is 8.25 ? Percentage = (8.25 - 0.75) � 10 = 75.0%
