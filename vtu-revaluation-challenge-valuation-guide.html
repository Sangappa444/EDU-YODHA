VTU Revaluation & Challenge Valuation Master Guide 2026

Complete step-by-step breakdown of applying for answer script photocopy, revaluation, and challenge valuation. Learn about fee structures and the 15% mark increase refund rule.

VTU Exams & Evaluation Updated for 2026 Batch • By EDU YODHA Academic Desk

If you are disappointed with your VTU Semester Examination marks or believe an answer script was evaluated unfairly, Visvesvaraya Technological University provides a formal 3-stage revaluation mechanism: Photocopy of Answer Script, Revaluation, and Challenge Valuation.

1. Difference Between Revaluation & Challenge Valuation

Feature Revaluation (RV) Challenge Valuation (CV)
Who Evaluates? Evaluated by a second independent evaluator assigned by VTU. Evaluated in front of a committee involving senior faculty/subject experts.
Prerequisite Can apply directly or after viewing script photocopy. Mandatory to obtain Answer Script Photocopy first.
Approx. Fee (per subject) ₹400 - ₹500 ₹5,000 (Refundable if marks increase substantially)
Fee Refund Rule Non-refundable. 50% - 80% fee refunded if marks increase by >15%.

2. Step-by-Step Revaluation Application Process

  1. Notification Announcement: VTU issues an official circular on `vtu.ac.in` opening the revaluation portal (usually within 7-10 days after main results).
  2. College Portal Registration: Contact your college's exam section or login through the official VTU pre-examination portal (`prexam.vtu.ac.in`).
  3. Subject Selection: Choose the specific subject codes for which you wish to apply for Photocopy and/or Revaluation.
  4. Online Fee Payment: Pay the prescribed fees online to your college exam desk or through the official payment gateway before the deadline.
  5. Result Announcement: Revaluation results are published within 3 to 4 weeks on the VTU results server.

3. VTU Mark Update & Award Rules

Rule of Higher Marks Awarded:

If the revaluation marks are higher than the original marks, the higher marks are updated in your Grade Card. If the revaluation marks are lower, VTU retains your original higher marks!
